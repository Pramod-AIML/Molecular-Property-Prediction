1. Aqueous Solubility (LogS) & BCS Classification

Under the Biopharmaceutics Classification System (BCS), candidates with LogS > -4.0 (or > 0.1 mg/mL) are considered sufficiently soluble to preclude dissolution rate-limited bioavailability. Our Extra Trees model achieves an MAE of 0.4628, lower than the standard inter-laboratory experimental variance (±0.50 Log units).

2. Clinical Trial Safety Risk (ClinTox) & p53 Genotoxicity

The ClinTox model screens for compounds that failed FDA Phase I/II clinical trials due to human organ toxicities. The Tox21 p53 cellular pathway model detects genotoxic liabilities triggering the p53 tumor suppressor response. Dual negative clearance is required before advancing candidates to in-vivo safety pharmacology.

3. Pan-Assay Interference Compounds (PAINS)

PAINS motifs (e.g. rhodanines, quinones, hydroxyphenylhydrazones) display non-specific target inhibition through redox cycling, covalent protein adduction, or aggregation. Automating PAINS filtration prevents costly false-positive wet-lab follow-up assays.
